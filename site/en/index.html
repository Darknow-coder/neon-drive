THE GAME

A fast racing game built for phone and PC

NEON//DRIVE is a top-down racer set in a city of neon. A race takes a few minutes: perfect for a quick run, or for playing for hours.

Drift, boost and overtake

Brake, steer, and your car slides into a drift. A drift assist (you can turn it off) keeps you off the walls while you learn. Every drift fills your boost gauge: you choose the right moment to fire it.

  • Rocket start if you hit the gas right on green
  • 5 opponents who really fight for position
  • A quick tutorial to learn everything

A career with real rivals

5 chapters, 5 rivals with their own personality and story. Win the events, earn up to 75 stars and beat each boss in a duel.

13 tracks and weather that changes everything

Neon boulevards, a snowy pass, a desert, a cherry-blossom avenue, a stormy peak… Every track has its own mood, and the weather changes the grip: rain and snow are slippery, fog hides the corners, gusts of wind push you around.

13 cars to customize

Every car handles differently. Upgrade them, then head to the workshop: any color you like, finishes, decals, numbers, boost trails and drift smoke.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is NEON//DRIVE really free?

Yes, completely. No ads and no real-money purchases: you unlock everything by playing.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs right in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge…), on phone or computer.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, with touch controls. Hold your phone sideways (landscape).

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. As long as you don't clear your browser data, your progress stays.

Do I need an account?

No. For the online leaderboard you just pick a nickname: no email, no password.

Ready to hit the track?

A race takes a few minutes. Your first one starts in 10 seconds.
