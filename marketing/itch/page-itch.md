## Description

```
Race through a city of neon in a fast, top-down arcade racer you can play right here, in your browser.

▶ PLAY FREE: no download, no ads, no account needed.
📱 Works on phone (touch controls) and PC (keyboard or gamepad).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🏁 WHAT'S INSIDE

• 13 tracks: neon boulevards, a snowy mountain pass, a desert, a cherry-blossom avenue, a storm-hit peak…
• Weather that changes the grip: rain, snow, fog, lightning storms, sandstorm gusts
• 13 cars to unlock, each with its own handling
• CAREER: 5 chapters, 5 rivals with their own story, 75 stars to earn
• RANKED RACES: climb from Bronze to Elite league
• CUP, ELIMINATION (last car out every lap) and the 20-car GIGA RUSH event
• TIME TRIAL: race your own ghost, earn medals, and challenge your friends' ghosts
• DAILY CHALLENGE, daily and weekly missions, a season pass with free rewards
• WORKSHOP: paint every car the way you like, with decals, numbers, boost trails and drift smoke

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎮 CONTROLS

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to drive · Shift = boost · Space = brake · R = back on track · P / Esc = pause
Gamepad: supported
Phone: on-screen buttons (turn the phone sideways)

Drift tip: brake + turn at the start of a corner, then hold the direction. A drift assist helps you stay off the walls (you can turn it off in Settings).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🌐 ONLINE (optional)

World leaderboards and friends' time-trial ghosts. No email, no password: you just pick a nickname. Your progress is saved on your device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💬 FEEDBACK WANTED!

This is my first game and I'm improving it every week. Tell me what you liked, what was too hard, or what broke: there's a feedback button right in the game (💬, top of the main menu), or leave a comment below. I read everything!

Available in English and French.
```
